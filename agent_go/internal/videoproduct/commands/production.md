Before generating anything, work through the checkpoints in `video-creation`
with me:

1. Confirm what this video is — audience, message, platform, aspect ratio, and
   roughly how long it should be.
2. Tell me which provider keys are actually configured, and what this piece is
   likely to cost: how many paid generation calls it implies, and what happens
   to that number if shots need re-prompting. Ask me for a ceiling.
3. Ask whether any character, presenter, or product has to recur across shots,
   because that decides which models are even usable.

Ask these together, once, in plain language — not as a form. Then stop and wait
for my answers before spending anything.
